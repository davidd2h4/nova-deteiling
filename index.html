04 — The studio

Detailing is more than cleaning.

NOVA DETAILING is a studio for owners who can tell a washed car from a finished one. We work panel by panel, with professional compounds, coatings, and interior chemistry chosen for the surface in front of us — not a preset that gets rushed through the door.

Precision is the work. Premium products are how the finish lasts. The aim is protection you can still see months later: clearer paint, a cabin that feels looked after, and a vehicle that stays that way.
